## 📁 Projects

### 1. 🏦 Bank Analysis

**Project Overview**

The Bank Analysis project explores customer financial data to understand customer demographics, account balances, banking behavior, and relationships between financial features. The project focuses on exploratory data analysis and visualizations to identify patterns, distributions, and potential business opportunities.

**🎯 Objectives**
- Analyze customer demographics and financial characteristics.
- Understand the distribution of categorical and numerical features.
- Compare customer characteristics across genders.
- Identify relationships between financial variables.
- Derive actionable business insights from data.

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**
- Python
- Pandas — data manipulation and analysis
- NumPy — numerical operations
- Matplotlib — data visualization
- Seaborn — statistical visualization
- Jupyter Notebook — analysis and documentation

**🔍 Work Performed**

**1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
- Explored the dataset structure, columns, and data types.
- Analyzed categorical and numerical features.
- Examined customer demographics and financial attributes.

**2. Categorical Feature Analysis**
- Analyzed category frequencies for nationality, occupation, fee structure, loyalty classification, properties owned, risk weighting, and other categorical features.
- Created professional bar charts to visualize category distributions.
- Compared male and female customer counts across categorical features.

**3. Numerical Feature Analysis**
- Analyzed the distributions of:
  - Superannuation Savings
  - Estimated Income
  - Credit Card Balance
  - Bank Loans
  - Bank Deposits
  - Checking Accounts
  - Saving Accounts
  - Foreign Currency Account
  - Business Lending
- Created gender-wise histograms with density curves.
- Identified positively skewed distributions and potential high-value observations.

**4. Correlation Analysis**
- Created a correlation heatmap for numerical financial features.
- Identified strong positive relationships, including:
  - Bank Deposits and Checking Accounts: **0.84**
  - Bank Deposits and Saving Accounts: **0.75**
- Investigated relationships between financial variables and considered potential multicollinearity.

**5. Business Insights**
- Explored customer financial patterns and differences across customer groups.
- Identified potential opportunities for customer segmentation and targeted banking services.
- Highlighted the need for further investigation of unusually high financial values.
- Considered how correlated features may affect future predictive modeling.

**📌 Key Findings**
- Several financial variables exhibit right-skewed distributions, indicating that a smaller group of customers has substantially higher financial values.
- Bank Deposits and Checking Accounts show a strong positive correlation of 0.84.
- Bank Deposits and Saving Accounts show a strong positive correlation of 0.75.
- Male and female customer distributions appear broadly similar across many analyzed features, although some categorical differences warrant further investigation.
- Correlation does not imply causation, and further statistical analysis is required to validate business hypotheses.
